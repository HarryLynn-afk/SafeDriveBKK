What We Do

Government agencies track accidents after they happen. RoadRisk Bangkok predicts where accidents are most likely to occur before you drive there — using 2024 Bangkok accident data to identify persistent danger zones across the city.

Just 5% of Bangkok's area (126 zones) accounts for 88.4% of all accidents. Drivers get real-time risk alerts, fast-vs-safe route comparison, and a conversational safety assistant — all from one ML model.

Technology

🤖 XGBoost ML Model

  • 44 engineered features per 1 km² zone
  • Severity, spatial, temporal & vehicle mix
  • Trained on 2,089 records, tested on 569
  • 17.5× lift over baseline

📝 Thai NLP Pipeline

  • pythainlp tokenization on accident reports
  • TF-IDF + Logistic Regression classifier
  • 6 cause categories, 99.5% cross-validation accuracy (training data)
  • NLP features feed directly into XGBoost

🗺️ Data Visualization

  • Interactive Folium risk heatmap
  • 12+ Chart.js analytics charts
  • Grab-style trip planner with route comparison
  • Real-time driving simulation with voice alerts

Data, Trust & Ethics

📊 Data Source

  • Thai Ministry of Transport, 2024
  • 23,952 nationwide records
  • 2,669 Bangkok accidents (91.5% GPS accuracy)
  • Public, official government data

🔒 Privacy

  • No personal data collected or stored
  • GPS processed in-memory only
  • No user tracking or identification
  • Public aggregate statistics only

⚠️ Limitations

  • Based on 2024 historical data only
  • Not a guarantee of future safety
  • 1 km grid resolution — local variations exist
  • Not for legal or insurance use

📊 Known Biases

  • Reporting bias: not all accidents reported
  • GPS accuracy: 8.5% missing location data
  • Urban bias: Bangkok coverage only
  • Temporal: 2024 data, infrastructure may have changed

✓ Route planning awareness for daily commuters

✓ Public awareness campaigns for high-risk areas

❌ Inappropriate Use Cases
