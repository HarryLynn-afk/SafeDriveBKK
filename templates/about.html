What We Do

Government agencies track accidents after they happen. RoadRisk Bangkok predicts where accidents are most likely to occur before you drive there, using 2024 Bangkok accident data to identify persistent danger zones across the city.

Drivers get real-time risk alerts during navigation, a fast-vs-safe route comparison, and a conversational safety assistant — all powered by the same underlying ML model.

Just 5% of Bangkok's area (126 zones) accounts for 88.4% of all accidents — these are the zones we track.

Key Discovery

Severity matters more than count. XGBoost revealed that a zone with 4 severe accidents (deaths, serious injuries) is more dangerous than one with 6 minor accidents. This insight caught the 2 danger zones Random Forest missed.
