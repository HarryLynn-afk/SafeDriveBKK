What We Do

Government agencies usually react after a crash. SafeDrive BKK predicts where accidents are most likely to happen before someone drives there, using 2024 Bangkok crash data to identify persistent danger zones across the city.

Just 126 high-risk zones capture 88.4% of mapped crashes. Drivers get route-aware safety guidance, risk visualization, and conversational answers from a single data system built around that concentration.

Technology

XGBoost ML Model

  • 44 engineered features per 1 km² zone
  • Severity, spatial, temporal, and vehicle mix inputs
  • Trained on 2,089 records and tested on 569
  • 17.5× lift over baseline zone selection

Thai NLP Pipeline

  • pythainlp tokenization on accident reports
  • TF-IDF with Logistic Regression classification
  • Six cause categories with 99.5% cross-validation accuracy on training data
  • NLP outputs feed directly into the zone risk model

Product Surfaces

  • Interactive Folium citywide risk heatmap
  • Analytics dashboards built with Chart.js
  • Trip Planner route comparison with safety context
  • Safety Assistant grounded in Bangkok crash data

Data, Trust & Ethics

Data Source

  • Thai Ministry of Transport, 2024
  • 23,952 nationwide records in the source release
  • 2,669 Bangkok crashes with 91.5% GPS coverage
  • Public official government data

Privacy

  • No personal data is collected or stored
  • GPS is processed in memory for route guidance only
  • No user tracking or identity profiles
  • Outputs rely on public aggregate crash patterns

Limitations

  • Predictions are based on 2024 historical data
  • The system does not guarantee future safety
  • 1 km grid resolution still hides local street variation
  • Not intended for legal or insurance decisions

Known Biases

  • Not every crash is reported into the source system
  • 8.5% of records lack precise GPS coordinates
  • Coverage is specific to Bangkok urban conditions
  • Infrastructure conditions may have changed since 2024

Appropriate Use Cases

✓ Route planning awareness for daily commuters

✓ Fleet safety decision support for logistics operators

✓ Infrastructure planning and resource targeting

✓ Public communication around concentrated high-risk areas

Inappropriate Use Cases

✗ Discriminatory insurance pricing based on location

✗ Restricting access or stigmatizing neighborhoods

✗ Replacing safe driving judgment and legal road rules

✗ Determining liability in an accident case
